Who Are We?

A construction company based on the principle, "Trust is Built, not Given." Instead of just telling customers to trust their work, the company demonstrates its dedication in all aspects: by the plans, the inspections, the communications, and the work itself. The business offers construction, renovation, project management, and design services for residential, commercial, and community projects, including challenging, unconventional, and otherwise difficult-to-develop sites and complex infrastructure projects. Salig Construction & Co. doesn't want to just get projects done — it wants to build a better future, deliver long-lasting value, and forge community opportunities wherever possible.

Our Mission

Salig Construction & Co. is committed to provide quality workmanship and customer service while upholding the highest standards of professionalism, honesty, fairness, and integrity in all relationships with our clients and employees, to adapt a disciplined and consistent approach to every project regardless of its size and/or scale ensuring excellence and reliability in all aspects of our work, and to grow by continuously providing valuable, significant, and impactful services and solutions, expanding into new areas that strengthens our expertise and align with customer’s needs.

Trust

Our Vision

To become a pioneer in the journey of construction and a leading innovator in creating livelihoods; building a future founded on trust, strengthened by quality, and sustained through infrastructure that improves lives and creates opportunities for generations to come.

We believe in building with purpose, creating lasting impact, and shaping spaces that strengthen the communities around us.

  • 1.

    Innovate and Pioneer

    Identify and adopt modern, profitable engineering techniques while ensuring that projects are effective, efficient, and sustainable.

  • 2.

    Synergize and Transcend

    Harness our company’s collective expertise and strengths to develop solutions that surpass conventional standards and effectively address our client’s needs.

  • 3.

    Establish Excellence

    Uphold excellence as the standard across our services and operations delivering quality projects, exceeding client expectations, and building a reputation that earns trust and attracts future opportunities.

  • 4.

    Progress with Purpose

    Contribute to the well-being of the communities where we operate by pursuing economically sustainable projects that create lasting value for society, the environment, and future generations.
